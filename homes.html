Open Roles

Who We're Looking For

Qualified Professional (QP)

Care Coordination

At Jones Family Care Solutions, our Qualified Professionals ensure that individuals with IDD receive consistent, high-quality support tailored to their goals and daily needs. Our QPs focus on care coordination, service oversight, and compliance, working closely with families and support teams to promote independence and stability.

Direct Support Professional (DSP)

Direct Care

Our Direct Support Professionals provide hands-on assistance to help individuals with IDD build meaningful lives. DSPs promote independence by supporting daily living activities and social engagement in a safe and respectful environment.

Program Coordination

Operations

Our program coordinators and caregivers are experienced, compassionate, and dedicated to providing the highest quality of care. They undergo rigorous training and background checks to ensure they are qualified to provide the care our clients need.
