What We Offer

Home Health Care Services

Licensed Group Home

Our Licensed Group Home offers a small, family-style residence with round-the-clock staffing and structure. Residents typically have private or semi-private bedrooms, shared living spaces, and a predictable daily routine designed around individual goals.

  • Supervision & safety monitoring
  • Medication administration
  • Personal care assistance
  • Transportation & community inclusion
  • Skill-building: cooking, housekeeping, money management
  • Behavior supports & care coordination

Ideal for: Individuals who benefit from 24/7 support in a stable, structured environment.

Alternative Family Living Home

Alternative Family Living (AFL) — also called a host-home model — matches individuals with a carefully screened, trained caregiver or family who provides support in their home. AFL offers a warm, natural home setting with highly individualized attention (often 1:1 or 1:2).

  • Personal care & medication assistance
  • Medical & therapy appointment coordination
  • Community participation & transportation
  • Daily living skill development
  • Person-centered plan guidance
  • Regular oversight, training & monitoring

Ideal for: People who prefer a family-based environment with consistent relationships and flexible, individualized routines.
