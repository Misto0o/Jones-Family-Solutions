Our Vision for Jones Family Care Solutions

At Jones Family Care Solutions LLC, we are dedicated to guiding individuals on their journey toward independence and well-being through compassionate and personalized placement services.

We connect clients with the most suitable care environments — ranging from AFL Homes and Group Homes to structured Day Programs — ensuring each path promotes independence, dignity, and holistic well-being. Our experienced team collaborates closely with individuals, families, and care providers to create safe, stable, and supportive placements that empower long-term success. Whether you're navigating residential care or exploring community-based programs, Jones Family Care Solutions is your trusted partner in finding the right support.

What We Provide

Quality-Driven Care

At Jones Family Care Solutions, quality and dignity are at the heart of everything we do. We are committed to upholding the highest standards in supporting individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

Placement Services

We connect you to the right Alternative Family Living (AFL) Home, understanding admission requirements, and ensuring safe, compliant care. Our placements range from AFL homes and group homes to community-based programs tailored to individual needs.

Resource Center

Our resource center provides guidance on finding the right care environment, medication management strategies, and caregiver training materials. We make it easy to access best practices, educational tools, and community-based solutions designed to improve care quality.
