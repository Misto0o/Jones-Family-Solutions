Our Vision for Royals House of Care

At Royals House of Care, we are dedicated to guiding individuals on their journey toward mental wellness through compassionate and personalized placement services.

We connect clients with the most suitable care environments ranging from AFL Homes and Group Homes to structured Day Programs ensuring each path promotes independence, dignity, and holistic well-being. Our experienced team collaborates closely with individuals, families, and care providers to create safe, stable, and supportive placements that empower long-term success. Whether you're navigating residential care or exploring community-based programs, Royals House of Care is your trusted partner in finding the right support for lasting mental health.

What We Provide

CQL Accredited Care

At Royals House of Care, quality and dignity are at the heart of everything we do. We have achieved CQL (Council on Quality and Leadership) accreditation, a mark of excellence in supporting individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

CQL ACCREDITED 2025

Placement Services

We connect you to the right Alternative Family Living (AFL) Home, understanding admission requirements, and ensuring safe, compliant care. Our placements range from AFL homes and group homes to community-based programs tailored to individual needs.

Resource Center

Our resource center provides guidance on finding the right care environment, medication management strategies, and caregiver training materials. We make it easy to access best practices, educational tools, and community-based solutions designed to improve care quality.
